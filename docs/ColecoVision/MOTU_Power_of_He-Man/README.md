# Masters of the Universe: The Power of He-Man – ColecoVision Prototype

**Historical Preservation Archive, Development Blueprints, Prototype Hardware & Screen Analysis**

---

## Overview

In 1983, following the successful commercial releases of *Masters of the Universe: The Power of He-Man* on the Intellivision and Atari 2600, **Mattel Electronics** developed an official conversion for the **ColecoVision** (designated as catalog item `#7759`). Utilizing the ColecoVision's Texas Instruments TMS9918A Video Display Processor (VDP) and Zilog Z80A CPU, the game featured significantly higher visual fidelity than the Atari 2600 edition and smoother multi-directional scrolling than the original Intellivision game.

Although featured prominently in Mattel's internal 1983 dealer catalogs and scheduled for commercial distribution, the title was abruptly cancelled following the catastrophic video game industry crash of 1983–1984, which culminated in the closure of Mattel Electronics in early 1984.

The ColecoVision prototype remained hidden for two decades until August 2003, when the **Blue Sky Rangers** (the collective of former Mattel Electronics programmers led by Keith Robinson) brought a playable EPROM prototype board and original hand-drawn design documents to the **Classic Gaming Expo (CGE 2003)** in Las Vegas, Nevada. This archive consolidates the dealer catalog listings, original pre-production artwork, hardware photographs, title screen transcriptions, and gameplay frame captures preserved from the prototype.

## 5. Gameplay Mechanics & Direct Frame Captures

Like its Intellivision predecessor, *The Power of He-Man* on ColecoVision is structured into two distinct, alternating phases of play:

---

### 5.1 Phase 1: Wind Raider Aerial Flight

In Phase 1, He-Man pilots the Wind Raider across the skies of Eternia toward Castle Grayskull, navigating through Skeletor's aerial and ground defenses.

#### Direct Digital Capture (`post-25956-0-56162000-1305418662.gif`)

![Wind Raider Aerial Flight Direct Capture](post-25956-0-56162000-1305418662.gif)

#### Off-Screen CRT Display Capture (`post-25956-0-34757200-1305418649.jpg`)

![Wind Raider Aerial Flight CRT Capture](post-25956-0-34757200-1305418649.jpg)

* **Visual & Mechanical Analysis:**
  * **Wind Raider:** Rendered in green, pink, and white sprite layers. The craft can maneuver vertically and horizontally across the screen while adjusting flight speed.
  * **Combat Mechanics:** The Wind Raider fires forward-projecting white ION Cannon laser beams to vaporize Skeletor's red/orange patrol ships in mid-air, and can drop downward MUTRON bombs to destroy ground targets.
  * **Parallax Scrolling:** The background features multi-layered scrolling mountains and hills with white-capped peaks and green foliage, providing a sense of speed and depth that was widely compared to Irem's *Moon Patrol*.
  * **Ground Threats:** Purple enemy soldiers march along the green terrain below while anti-aircraft ground batteries loose flak projectiles upward.
  * **HUD Telemetry Bar:** Four operational metrics are docked along the lower green boundary:
    * **Leftmost Counter (`20` / `29`):** Remaining flight distance / waypoints to Castle Grayskull.
    * **Second Counter (`48` / `49`):** Wind Raider energy / fuel gauge.
    * **Third Counter (`3` / `6`):** Remaining lives.
    * **Rightmost Counter (`99200` / `0`):** Accumulated score.

---

### 5.2 Phase 2: Castle Grayskull Infiltration

Upon surviving the aerial approach, He-Man reaches the fortress itself to confront Skeletor.

#### Direct Digital Capture (`post-25956-0-58459200-1305418668.gif`)

![Castle Grayskull Infiltration Direct Capture](post-25956-0-58459200-1305418668.gif)

* **Visual & Mechanical Analysis:**
  * **Castle Grayskull Facade:** Rendered in vibrant lime-green and teal stone brickwork, closely matching the production layout blueprint (`EVW2FAqXYAM1Pkh.jpg`). The fortress towers feature medieval crenellations, arched parapet windows, and the iconic skull face with glowing red eyes in the eye sockets.
  * **Entryway & Floor:** He-Man (yellow and white sprite) stands upon a blue hexagonal-tiled foreground platform spanning the entrance moat.
  * **Combat Objective:** He-Man must advance toward the gaping skull jawbridge, dodging magical energy barriers and blast walls, before entering the castle to confront Skeletor in direct hand-to-hand combat.
