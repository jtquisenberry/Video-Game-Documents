## INTRODUCTION

This walkthrough / FAQ / guide is for the game "He-Man: The Most Powerful 
Game in the Universe". The guide was written while playing the Android 
version 1.0.3.

There is also a version for iOS, and it is believed that nearly all of
this guide is applicable to that version.

"He-Man" is a 2-D beat-em-up with platforming elements.

The visual style reflects the 198x character designs rather than the 
200x designs.

## CONTROLS AND ABILITIES

### BASICS

There are two control schemes.

#### Gamepad: Default
This scheme relies on swipe gestures.

"Touch/Drag to MOVE."
"Tap to ATTACK!"
"Swipe up to JUMP."


#### Gamepad: Classic
In this scheme, four buttons are displayed on the screen.

Touch left arrow or right arrow to move.
Touch J to jump.
Touch A to attack.


### HE-MAN
He-Man may be upgraded to perform several special attacks.

#### Thunder Punch
Swipe <up> while standing still to activate the Thunder Punch. 
To unleash the upgraded Thunder Punch, swipe <up> while standing still or 
in mid-air.

#### Axe Throw
To unleash the Axe Throw, swipe <right> while standing still.

#### Power Slice
#### Overhead Combo
#### Wall Crusher

#### Tornado Spin
Swipe <down> while standing still to unleash the Tornado Spin.
Swipe <down> while standing still to unleash the improved Tornado Spin.

#### Aerial Smash
To unleash this ability, swipe <up> while airborne.


### SHE-RA
She-Ra may be upgrade to perform several special attacks.

#### Glitter Wave
Swipe <up> while standing still to unleash the She-ra's Glitter Wave.

#### Honor Dodge
To avoid enemy attacks, swipe <right> in the air.

#### Air Dance
Swipe <right> while standing or in the air to dash into enemies.

#### Power Thrust
To avoid enemy attacks, swipe <left> while on the ground.

#### Stiletto Stomp
Swipe <down> while in the air to stomp on enemies from above.


## STRATEGIES

### GENERALLY

* Collecting crystals is a priority. It may take many playthroughs to 
  gather enough crystals to unlock all of the special attacks.

* Destroy objects such as pillars, crystals and blocks whenever possible. 
  You may receive hearts or crystals. Attacking objects also increases the
  value of the combo gauge.
  
* Work for combos. In the upper-left corner is a combo meter. As you 
  score multiple hits on enemies or objects in a short period of time, the
  meter is filled. As the meter fills, the crystal multiplier is increased,
  increasing the rate at which crystals are obtained.
  
* Unlock upgrades. Heart containers and special abilities provide advantages
  to He-Man and She-Ra.
  
* Power of Grayskull and Summon Tokens. The Power of Grayskull provides 
  temporary invincibility. Summon Tokens allow Man-at-Arms or Swift Wind to
  fight for you temporarily. While they are fighting, He-Man and She-Ra are
  not present to take damage. While useful at any time, these tokens are
  especially useful during boss fights.
  
* Consider purchasing Lore Finder and Gem Doubler for 1.99 USD each. 
  Lore Finder: "A glowing icon will indicate when He-Man is near a lore 
  fragment."
  Gem Doubler: "Double the value of gems picked up by your hero."
  
* When your character has jumped into the air, the character will not fall
  as long as you keep attacking with the sword quickly. This is a way to 
  avoid falling into enemies on the ground. It is also a good way to stay
  in position to attack flying enemies.
 
  
## BOSSES

Note: When landing a successful attack, gems may fall from the boss. Much of 
the time, the gems will land on the far side of the boss, meaning that you
must take damage in order to retrieve them. This makes achieving a 
three-star rating extra-challenging. 

The easiest way to obtain a three-star rating is to call upon Man-at-Arms or
Swift Wind. All gems dropped due to a hit by Man-at-Arms's blaster are 
collected immediately. 

While using a summon, He-Man and She-Ra are not on the screen and cannot take
damage. The summon is more effective at dispatching bosses than the
Power of Grayskull.



#B001
### BEASTMAN

In this level, you must run from Beastman. When you can go no farther,
jump on the ledge on the right side of the screen. Beastman's rampage
stops at that ledge. Note that you should not remain on the ledge after
the battle starts, since Beastman can jump onto it.

When the battle starts, jump down from the ledge and watch where Beastman
lands. Go to him and attack repeatedly. When he raises his arms, move 
away; he is preparing to take a swipe at you. When the swipe is finished,
move in for another attack. 

Beastman will jump high into the air. A red arrow appears on the screen
to show where he will land. Move a little bit away from the arrow to
avoid Beastman's attack, but stay close enough that you can get in an
attack of your own. 

Beastman will perform several small jumps towards you. Back away to 
avoid them. If you reach the edge of the screen, double-jump over Beastman.

Beastman may start running back and forth. Stay ahead of him and jump
on the ledge at either side of the field.

When you have depleted Beastman's health, continue to the right. Beastman
will follow you and leap onto the bridge. The bridge will collapse and 
Beastman will be covered in mud and unable to move.


#B002
### KING HSSS

When the battle starts, go toward King Hsss and attack rapidly. A green
light will appear in the mouth of one of his snake heads. That indicates that
King Hsss is preparing to shoot a projectile toward the ground. Move 
as far away from Hsss as you can. When the projectile hits the ground, it
will form the shape of a snake head and move along the floor towards you.
Jump over it. Hsss may do this another one or two times. 

If one of Hsss's heads pulls back without showing a green light, that 
indicates that Hsss is preparing to launch a series of horizontal
pulses. Move away from Hsss and jump over the low-flying ones.

When you have depleted Hsss's health, he loses his balance and collapses
under the weight of his snake heads. 


#B003
### HORDAK

When the battle starts, go to Hordak and begin attacking. Do not stand too
close to Hordak because he will transform his arm into a rocket launcher and
fire rockets at you. You need to allow enough space between him and you to 
let you destroy the rockets as you attack Hordak. 

If Hordak starts to glow, he will transform into a large rocket. Jump 
straight up immediately. Follow behind Hordak's rocket form and attack him 
once he regains his normal form.

After a few transformations into a rocket, Hordak will transform into a
hovercraft. If you were to jump toward Hordak, you would jump into the 
hovercraft and take damage. This is why you should jump straight up. The
hovercraft form drops grenades. If you stay under the hovercraft, Hordak
will alternate trajectories - towards the right, towards the left, and so
on. Knowing this simple pattern, it is easy to dodge the grenades.

Once you have depleted Hordak's health, he transforms into a crate. Go to
the crate and attack. You will punch the crate toward the screen and win
the battle.


#B004
### MER-MAN

Prior to the beginning of the battle, when Skeletor summons Mer-Man, notice
that when Mer-Man jumps out of the water, he makes a small disturbance in
the water. 

When the battle starts, Mer-Man jumps to one side of the screen. A red arrow
indicates which side. Mer-Man attacks with energy from his trident. Jump 
the energy blasts. 

If Mer-Man points his trident at you, he will charge you. Jump away. Expect
that Mer-Man will jump into the water after his attack.

Eventually, Mer-Man will jump away, and the ground will rumble. His giant
green fish will be swimming through. Double-jump over the fish. If the ground
shakes again, prepare to double-jump over the fish again. 

The water below will show a large disturbance. Jump out of the way. The fish
is about to make a vertical leap. Any time there is a large disturbance, the
fish will jump. Any time there is small disturbance, Mer-Man will jump. If 
you are expecting Mer-Man, jump away but not too far away. Attack Mer-Man when
he arrives. 

Sometime a small disturbance indicates that Mer-Man will jump, but not very 
high. He is trying to attack your feet with his trident. Jump away but not
very far, in case Mer-Man jumps onto the platform. 

When you have depleted Mer-Man's health, Mer-Man's giant fish swallows him.


#B005
### TRAP JAW

At the beginning of the battle, Trap Jaw is in his pulverizer machine and
swaying back and forth. Walk left, staying a little ahead of the machine.
Soon, the machine will drop, and you should walk over to the machine and 
attack it. 

Trap Jaw will raise the machine into the air again and fire a
continuous beam toward the ground. Go all the way to the right wall. The 
beam will not get you if you are against a wall. After the beam stops,
run under the machine, as Trap Jaw will try to land it on your head. That
he can do when you are against a wall. Wait for the machine to land again
and attack it. 

Trap Jaw may cause the machine to sway and fall several more times. The key
is not to be under it when it lands. When the beam is active, the key is
to make it to one of the walls. 

When you have Trap Jaw's health nearly depleted, be sure to be as close to
him as you can when you administer the finishing blow. This increases the
likelihood of getting crystals before the game takes control of your 
character from you. 


#B006
### SKELETOR

At the beginning of the battle, Skeletor's primary attack is to propel flames
along the ground towards you. You should move toward the crawling flames and 
jump over them. Once you have cleared the flames, attack Skeletor. As Skeletor 
prepares to propel the flames, don't be too far away. You will be able to 
attack Skeletor only briefly after he propels the flames.

When attacking Skeletor, position yourself so that only the tip of the sword
strikes him. If you get too close, Skeletor will bash you with the Havok 
Staff, depleting 1/2 of a heart.

After you deplete approximately 1/4 of Skeletor's health, he will generate two
illusions of himself. The illusions are partially transparent. If you attack
an illusion, Skeletor will attack you. If you attack the real Skeletor, then
Skeletor takes damage and then teleports to one side of the area.

Chase Skeletor to the side of the screen he has chosen. If he jumps, he will
fire fireballs at you. You want to be away from Skeletor a bit so that you can
dodge the fireballs.

If Skeletor does not jump, he will create pillars of flame from the ground. 
You want to stay close to Skeletor because he will not create the pillars
close to himself.

For the remainder of the battle, Skeletor relies on the tricks of the last 
three paragraphs. 


#B007
### SKELETOR 2

This battle is the first of a two-part battle against the final boss. This
battle is identical to the previous battle with Skeletor, except that you 
need only deplete 1/4 of his health. Skeletor performs only two attacks: 
the crawling flame attack, and an attack with the Havok Staff. 

Once you have depleted 1/4 of Skeletor's health, he transforms into 
Giga-Skeletor.


#B007
### GIGA-SKELETOR

You do not want to use just the basic sword attack for this battle. If you 
must, you need to attack Skeletor's head. This means you must perform a
double-jump to reach him. Skeletor is able to attack you as you jump and 
while you are in the air. 

A much better strategy is to use the Aerial Punch. He-Man in invulnerable
during the Aerial Punch, and the punch is more powerful than the basic
sword attack. Before performing an Aerial Punch, position yourself on the
ground between Skeletor's shoulder and head. 

It is best not to be right under his head to reduce the chances that you
will fall into Skeletor's body after the Aerial Punch. Skeletor moves 
back and forth between the center of the arena and the right side. When
Skeletor moves away from you, you are the least likely to take damage.
When Skeletor moves towards you, your risk of falling into his body is
greater.

Skeletor has a number of attacks, each of which the Aerial Punch helps you
avoid. 

Ground Pound: Skeletor hits the ground with his fist. The result is 
crawling flames emanating from both sides of the impact. Use the Aerial 
Punch just as Skeletor hits the ground to avoid the flames.

Havok Staff Beam: When Skeletor raises his staff, he is preparing to emit
a continuous beam. Skeletor does not aim close to himself. Staying near
him is a great way to avoid the beam. 

Hand Blast: When Skeletor raises his hand, he is preparing to emit a 
horizontal pulse of energy. If you are behind his hand, as you should
be to perform an Aerial Punch, you will not be in the path of the pulse.

If all else fails, even Giga-Skeletor is vulnerable to Man-at-Arms's beam
weapon. The beam weapon is your best chance to collect the gems you need
to obtain a three-star rating.

When Skeletor's health is defeated, you have beaten the game.

## LORE GUIDE

### DUNGEONS

#### Lore #L001
**Level:** 1-1
**Required:** Wall Crusher
**Reward:** Bio Skeletor
**Method:**
At the beginning of the level, turn left and attack the blocks. It is 
possible to walk through the wall. Do so, and then attack the large Horde
block. Walk left to grab the lore.
 
#### Lore #L002
**Level:** 1-1
**Required:** Nothing
**Reward:** Game Concept Art 9
**Method:**
About halfway through the level, there is a lore sitting on a floor made of
blocks. Walk to the lore and grab it. This is the point when the Sorceress 
states, "Upgrade your powers in store to find all the Lore Fragments." It is
not possible to skip this lore by jumping over it because the ceiling is low.

#### Lore #L003
**Level:** 1-2
**Required:** Super Thunder Punch or She-Ra
**Reward:** Comic Cover 14
**Method:**
About halfway through the level, rectangular platforms rise form a bridge 
over a gap. The lore is high in the air above the platforms. She-Ra can 
double-jump high enough to reach it. He-Man can double-jump and then use the
Super Thunder Punch to jump high enough.

#### Lore #L004
**Level:** 1-2
**Required:** Nothing
**Reward:** Product Art 3
**Method:**
This lore is acquired using the first of the invisible teleporters. 
The teleporter is located in the air to the right of the the bridge formed by
rectangular platforms (see #L003). Jump to reach the teleporter. The 
teleporter will bring you to the lore.

#### Lore #L005
**Level:** 1-2
**Required:** Wall Crusher or Super Thunder Punch or She-Ra
**Reward:** Comic Cover 7
**Method:**
Shortly after the Sorceress requires you to use the power of Grayskull 
for the first time, there is a wall held up by two destructible 
pillars. After that is a wall made of blocks. Slash the blocks and pass
through the opening. Jump to the next platform to the right. The lore is
on top. 

If you cannot break the blocks, go under the wall of blocks. It is 
possible to jump up to the platform with the lore. She-Ra can double-jump
high enough. He-Man can double-jump and then use the Super Thunder
Punch to jump high enough. Be sure not to destroy the destructible 
pillars.

#### Lore #L006
**Level:** 1-3
**Required:** Nothing
**Reward:** Comic Cover 1
**Method:**
At the beginning of the level, a Giant Skeleton Knight walks from the 
right side of the screen to the left. Jump down from the platform where you
start and walk left, following the Giant Skeleton Knight. Once off-screen, 
jump to obtain the lore.

#### Lore #L007
**Level:** 1-3
**Required:** Nothing
**Reward:** Comic Cover 11
**Method:** After facing the first Giant Skeleton Knight that you get to fight,
there is a wall held up by two destructible pillars. Double-jump onto the 
wall for the lore. 

#### Lore #L008
**Level:** 1-4
**Required:** Nothing
**Reward:** Game Concept Art 11
**Method:**
This lore is reached using an invisible teleporter. Near the 
beginning of the level is an area where a spiked ceiling rises and falls. 
At the farthest right portion of the spiked ceiling, wait for the ceiling
to rise fully. Then jump to activate the teleporter. The teleporter
takes you to the lore. Note that a Megaman-style door can be seen 
to the right of the invisible teleporter. If you pass through the door, 
you have gone to far.

#### Lore #L009
**Level:** 1-4
**Required:** Aerial Smash and Wall Crusher
**Reward:** Bio Hordak
**Method:**
About halfway through the level, the lore is beneath a bridge of destructible
blocks. Attack the blocks with the Aerial Smash and fall down to the lore.

#### Lore #L010
**Level:** 1-5
**Required:** Nothing
**Reward:** Product Art 5
**Method:**
This is the level where Beast-Man chases you. About 3/4 of the way through 
the level, the lore is seen floating in the air. Double-jump to grab it. 


### SNAKE MOUNTAIN

#### Lore #L011
**Level:** 2-1
**Required:** Super Wall Crusher
**Reward:** Game Concept Art 4
**Method:**
At the beginning of the level, shortly after volcanic rocks start falling from
the sky, you will see a down arrow made of crystals. Jump down and notice the
tough blocks to the left. Smash the tough blocks to reach the lore.
 
#### Lore #L012
**Level:** 2-1
**Required:** Super Wall Crusher
**Reward:** Game Concept Art 7
**Method:**
Near the end of the level, you will see a down arrow made of crystals. Jump
down and notice the tough blocks to the left. Smash the tough blocks to
reach the lore.

#### Lore #L013
**Level:** 2-1
**Required:** Nothing
**Reward:** Product Art 16
**Method:**
Instead of jumping down to retrieve #L012, continue to the right. Jump on 
the next wall to the right to reach the lore. If you are not quick enough,
volcanic rocks will destroy the wall. Then the only way to get the lore is 
to use the Super Thunder Punch or She-Ra. The lore is found before the next
wooden bridge.

#### Lore #L014
**Level:** 2-2
**Required:** Nothing
**Reward:** Product Art 8
**Method:**
About 2/3 of the way through the level, you will see a down arrow made of 
crystals. Jump down and notice that the ground vibrates a bit as you land. 
Run around the platform and jump onto it and double-jump onto it. 
Eventually, the platform will fall and reveal the lore.

#### Lore #L015
**Level:** 2-2
**Required:** Nothing (but Power of Grayskull is wise)
**Reward:** Game Concept Art 1
**Method:**
Near the end of the level, there is a long bridge that skeleton warriors 
jump onto. This bridge passes over a spiked floor. Backtrack to the left
and jump down onto the spikes. Run to the right to get the lore. You 
should expect to lose two hearts on the way to the lore. Without a total
of five hearts or the Power of Grayskull, you will probably die on the way
back. Of course, dying does not erase the lore. You can always just play
through the level again.

#### Lore #L016
**Level:** 2-3
**Required:** Nothing
**Reward:** Bio Teela
**Method:**
This level contains lava that rises and falls. Be sure to avoid the lava. 
At the beginning of the level is a long bridge that you must walk across. 
When the bridge ends, drop down, and turn left. Walk across another bridge 
to find the lore. 

#### Lore #L017
**Level:** 2-3
**Required:** Wall Smasher
**Reward:** Product Art 12
**Method:**
About halfway through the level, there is a point where you will walk right
along a stone bridge, drop down, walk left across another stone bridge, and
drop down onto a Horde box. To the left, the lore is behind a vertical wall
of destructible blocks.

#### Lore #L018
**Level:** 2-4
**Required:** Wall Smasher
**Reward:** Product Art 2
**Method:**
Near the beginning of the level you will fight Horde troopers with rocket
launchers. Then, you will see a horizontal line of destructible blocks 
above you. The line is two blocks tall. Go right to the end of the blocks.
Double-jump high enough to smash the top layer of blocks. Then jump on the 
lower layer. Beware of a bat on top of the blocks. Next see a vertical 
series of blocks to the right. Smash the blocks and jump through the 
opening to reach the lore.

#### Lore #L019
**Level:** 2-4
**Required:** Super Thunder Punch
**Reward:** Game Concept Art 16
**Method:**
About halfway through the level, there is a point where you will jump down 
between two spiked walls. Before that point, the is a ledge above you, and on
that ledge is the lore. It appears that the ledge is just a bit too high for
He-Man to double-jump onto. However, maybe someone will succeed. Until then, 
double-jump and use the Super Thunder Punch to reach the ledge. 

If you are playing as She-Ra, she can double-jump onto the ledge. The trick
is not to perform the second jump at the peak of the first jump, or you will 
hit your head on the top of the screen. Instead, perform the second jump
after falling a bit from the first jump.


### FRIGHT ZONE

#### Lore #L020
**Level:** 3-1
**Required:** Wall Smasher
**Reward:** Product Art 15
**Method:**
There is a point at which you will use a rising platform. Ride the platform
to the top of the wall to the right. There you will encounter two Horde
trooper grenadiers. Then, you will see a vertical line of destructible blocks
Repeatedly use the Aerial Punch to break the blocks, and the fall through
the gap. You will fall onto the lore. Below the lore is another rising 
platform, which will take you back to the top of the wall.
  
#### Lore #L021
**Level:** 3-1
**Required:** Nothing
**Reward:** Comic Cover 3
**Method:**
Shortly after #L020, you will come across a series of shaky platforms. Jump
from platform to platform to the right. If you are not quick, the platforms
will fall. When there are no more platforms to the right, double-jump to the
right to reach the lore. 

If you do not want to bother with the platforms, you can double-jump and use
the Super Thunder Punch to reach the lore. For She-Ra, double-jump is
sufficient.

#### Lore #L022
**Level:** 3-2
**Required:** Wall Smasher
**Reward:** Game Concept Art 3
**Method:**
This level has a vertical structure, and it features rising water that
you must outrun. To the left of the second rising platform is a wall of 
destructible blocks. Smash the blocks to reach the lore.

#### Lore #L023
**Level:** 3-2
**Required:** Super Wall Smasher
**Reward:** Product Art 7
**Method:**
Near the end of the level, to the right of a rising platform is a thick wall
of tough blocks. Smash the blocks to clear a path to the right and reach
the lore.
 
 
### CRYSTAL SEA
 
#### Lore #L024
**Level:** 4-1
**Required:** Nothing
**Reward:** Game Concept Art 6
**Method:**
After facing a series of blue wizards, you will jump down a long gap.
Soon, you will reach a wall with several landings that you can jump up. 
At the top, jump across a gap to the left. The lore is behind a destructible
giant crystal. If you pass a bridge made of six shaky ice platforms, 
you have gone too far.

#### Lore #L025
**Level:** 4-1
**Required:** Nothing
**Reward:** Comic Cover 10
**Method:**
Shortly after #L024, you will reach a bridge composed of six shaky ice
platforms. Walk across the bridge. Once on the other side, climb down the
landings. Once at the lowest level, double-jump across a gap to the left
to reach the lore.

#### Lore #L026
**Level:** 4-2
**Required:** Nothing
**Reward:** Bio Beast Man
**Method:**
Near the beginning of the level, you will not be able to progress until
after you have beaten several skeleton warriors and blue wizards. 
When you have done so, continue to the right. The lore is on the landing
of an ice wall.

#### Lore #L027
**Level:** 4-2
**Required:** Nothing
**Reward:** Comic Cover 13
**Method:**

   damage three times or fewer.


## GLITCHES AND CHEATS

### BATTLE ARMOR MODE

In version 1.0.1, after purchasing the Tornado Spin, it was possible to
activate Battle Armor mode at any time by swiping down. Normally, He-Man
must be standing still to execute a Tornado Spin, and this was impossible
without activating Battle Armor. However, if He-Man were walking, he
could activate the Tornado Spin. 

Battle Armor maximizes the gem multiplier. With five hearts, the maximum
multiplier is x6. With the Battle Armor glitch, it is easy to exceed 8,000
gems on level 5-1 consistently. Repeated use of Battle Armor also provided
effective invincibility. 

This glitch was removed in version 1.0.2.
