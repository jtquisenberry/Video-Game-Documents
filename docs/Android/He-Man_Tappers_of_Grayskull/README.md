## Overview

*He-Man: Tappers of Grayskull* is a mobile idle-tapper game developed and published by **Animoca Brands**, released simultaneously on Android and iOS on **August 31, 2016**. The game follows the classic idle-tapper genre format: players tap the screen to defeat enemies and accumulate resources, using those resources to hire helpers, upgrade characters, and unlock progression milestones — all set within the Masters of the Universe universe.

## About Animoca Brands

Animoca Brands is a Hong Kong–based game publisher and technology company known for licensed mobile games and, later, blockchain gaming. At the time of this release (2016), Animoca Brands was focused on traditional mobile game licensing, publishing titles based on major entertainment franchises. *He-Man: Tappers of Grayskull* is one of their licensed franchise titles from that period.

## Genre Context: Idle Tapper Games

The idle-tapper (or "clicker") genre reached peak popularity in the mid-2010s following the success of *Cookie Clicker* (2013) and *Clicker Heroes* (2014). By 2016, licensed idle-tappers built on established franchises were a common mobile publishing strategy. *Tappers of Grayskull* follows this pattern, applying the genre to the Masters of the Universe IP.

Typical idle-tapper mechanics expected in this game:

| Mechanic | Description |
|---|---|
| **Tap to damage** | Screen taps deal damage to on-screen enemies |
| **Heroes / helpers** | Hire MOTU characters to auto-attack on the player's behalf |
| **Upgrades** | Spend currency to increase tap damage and helper DPS |
| **Prestige / reset** | Optional restart loop to gain multiplier bonuses |
| **Milestones** | Story or achievement beats unlocked at resource thresholds |
